05

Frame Rules

Two Worlds · Morph · Big Type

Do

  • Morph shared elements between scenes.
  • Use a stepped typewriter for mono hooks.
  • Leave lots of empty cream around one huge word or statement.
  • Set captions as a pink pill with a yellow key phrase.
  • Arrive with expo.out easing.

Don't

  • Don't put candy colours in the spec world.
  • Don't mix both worlds in one frame.
  • Don't cut where a morph can carry the element.
  • Don't crowd the cream ground.
  • Don't use grey captions.
