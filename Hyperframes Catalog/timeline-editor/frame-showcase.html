Frame Rules

Flat · Heavy · One Orange

Do

  • Make the type the picture — one huge line or the timeline fills the canvas.
  • Vary line size and position; keep headlines off-centre.
  • Use orange once per frame: pill, send button or dragged clip.
  • Reveal words on the voice timestamps.
  • Keep the chat and limit card flat, white and hairline-bordered.

Don't

  • Don't centre every line.
  • Don't add grain or a second accent colour.
  • Don't shrink headlines into small decorated text.
  • Don't ground the page in anything but #F5F5F7.
  • Don't stack more than one gold word on a frame.
