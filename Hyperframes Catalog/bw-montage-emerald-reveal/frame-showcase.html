Frame Rules

Monochrome · Static Text · Green Release

Do

  • Grade every clip black-and-white until the interlude and reveal.
  • Set short white text, centred, static, while the footage moves.
  • Mix bold sans for key words with light serif italic for connectives.
  • Make every shot a literal metaphor for the word on screen.
  • Hard-cut every 1–2.5 seconds on the voice.

Don't

  • Don't colour the footage.
  • Don't animate text heavily over busy shots.
  • Don't introduce green before the interlude.
  • Don't use a caption card over the footage.
  • Don't exceed small text size except the wordmark.
