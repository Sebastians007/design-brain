Frame Rules

Glass · Highlight · Contained

Do

  • Keep outputs in a rounded light card; never full-bleed.
  • Hold short text beats about 0.3s.
  • Send motion leftward; use zoom for escalation.
  • Highlight the one phrase that matters in green.
  • Use green once per frame.

Don't

  • Don't show fake app chrome everywhere.
  • Don't use more than one accent colour per frame.
  • Don't full-bleed an output.
  • Don't drop the pattern ground behind glass cards.
  • Don't reuse a card without cropping tighter on the ladder.
