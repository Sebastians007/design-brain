05

Frame Rules

Pulse · Green · Restraint

Do

  • Make something pulse in time with the track on every frame.
  • Colour bars green when passed and grey once consumed; keep the playhead bright.
  • Set captions as bare white text at the bottom, one clause at a time.
  • Keep data panels dark with a 1px border and mono uppercase labels.

Don't

  • Don't add colour beyond green and the tiny red ticks.
  • Don't blur or glow the data.
  • Don't caption text that is already on screen.
  • Don't fill the frame with decoration; one data visual in the middle.
