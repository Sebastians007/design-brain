Frame Rules

Monochrome · Chrome-true

Do

  • Sync every click to a sound; keep the composer aligned across hard cuts.
  • Count the figure up with a proxy tween, then punch scale 1.06 and flash green.
  • Keep everything on the dark canvas except the single cream result tile.
  • Make cursor taps and typing visible.

Don't

  • Don't add warm colours outside the result tile.
  • Don't use generic purple or blue AI gradients.
  • Don't leave empty centred text without app chrome.
  • Don't colour anything beyond the connector mark and the success flash.
