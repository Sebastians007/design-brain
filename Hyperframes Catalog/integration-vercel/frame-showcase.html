Frame Rules

One Object · Black On White

Do

  • Keep one focal object per beat.
  • Spin the black solid with a power2.inOut feel.
  • Set the headline in tight black type on white.
  • Scale the plus in with a small overshoot.
  • Zoom-crop to the cursor and button during the recording.

Don't

  • Don't add fills or gradients to the white scenes.
  • Don't show more than two objects at once.
  • Don't add a second accent colour; the green mark is the only one.
  • Don't use heavy shadows beyond the soft shadow under the tiles.
