Frame Rules

One Idea · One Accent

Do

  • Keep text single-line and centred on white.
  • Use exactly one accent word per line; the showcase underlines it to hold AA contrast, the film colours the glyphs.
  • Put footage only in phone-ratio cards with hairline borders.
  • Fan cards out from the centre; stagger columns.
  • Let dark variant cards carry code with colour-coded values.

Don't

  • Don't use more than one accent per line.
  • Don't put footage full-bleed.
  • Don't colour the background.
  • Don't add grain or shaders.
  • Don't set accent words in pastel on white for small text.
