Frame Rules

Restraint · Silence · Photos

Do

  • Set tiny lowercase text, one phrase at a time, on cream.
  • Hold text beats for 1–3 seconds; let white space dominate.
  • Place the photo inside the sentence with words either side.
  • Let photography carry every colour.
  • Reserve heavy type for the final wordmark.

Don't

  • Don't add an accent colour.
  • Don't enlarge the poem type.
  • Don't hurry the holds.
  • Don't add shadows or decoration to the cream ground.
  • Don't use more than one heavy wordmark.
