Frame Rules

One World Per Frame

Do

  • Keep one idea per card and swap the copy about every two seconds.
  • Pop the key word in its pill with a slight overshoot.
  • Zoom the recording to the region the viewer should read.
  • Use green only on the dark sections.
  • Keep exactly one brand world in each frame.

Don't

  • Don't mix the lavender gradient and a black ground in one frame.
  • Don't show partner logos you don't have rights to.
  • Don't set grey connective words small.
  • Don't add shadows or glows to the dark title.
  • Don't leave a card without its key word.
