05

Frame Rules

Two Worlds · One Chip

Do

  • Hard-cut reel scenes in 1.5–2.5s, swapping the chip caption each cut.
  • Keep the chip in the same bottom-centre band every cut.
  • Keep UI scenes cream, ink and coral only.
  • Stamp the badge in with an impact scale.

Don’t

  • Don’t mix UI and reel styles in one frame.
  • Don’t put a chip over text that says the same thing.
  • Don’t use coral outside the Send button and cursor accents.
  • Don’t skip the serif title or wordmark close.
