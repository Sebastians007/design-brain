Frame Rules

Ground Moves First

Do

  • Keep the halftone dot field behind every scene; let it move before the foreground.
  • One big cream headline per scene.
  • Use one serif-italic or gradient accent word per line.
  • Set captions as dark pills, lower-centre.
  • Slide whole scenes as one stage.

Don't

  • Don't use a flat dark ground.
  • Don't use hard cuts.
  • Don't put two accent words in a line.
  • Don't add drop shadows.
  • Don't turn the ground into a terminal or slide deck.
