Frame Rules

Warm · Calm · Varied

Do

  • Keep the home base light and warm — cream paper.
  • Give each montage beat its own typeface and exactly one saturated colour.
  • Cut on spoken word boundaries; resolve entrances from a blur.
  • Set captions as dark translucent chips, bottom-centred.
  • Let silence breathe — economy of words.

Don't

  • Don't default to dark mode — black is only for the final ASCII close.
  • Don't reuse one font family for every world.
  • Don't start in silence.
  • Don't add film grain or heavy shadows.
  • Don't put more than one colour-shift word on a frame.
