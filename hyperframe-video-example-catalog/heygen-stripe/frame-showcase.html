05

Frame Rules

Contrast · One Idea · Green Only On Dark

Do

  • Alternate the three grounds; contrast between neighbours is the structure.
  • One headline per card, 6cqw or larger, in tight heavy type.
  • Keep green on the dark ground only (window ring, drum verb, prompt).
  • Hard-cut between scenes of the same ground; end on white.
  • Caption in white with a soft shadow, lower third, no box.

Don't

  • Don't put green or an extra accent hue on the sunburst.
  • Don't crossfade two similar-luma scenes.
  • Don't blur the 3D word drum.
  • Don't crowd a card with more than one idea.
  • Don't add a box behind captions.
