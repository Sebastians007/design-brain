05

Frame Rules

Black · Grey · Moving

Do

  • Keep something moving at all times: ticks, hatch, orbit, bar changes.
  • Use hard on/off cuts at measured times plus a few eased slides.
  • Keep black dominant and the palette to greys; put braces on at least one headline.
  • Keep furniture present on every frame.

Don't

  • Don't add colour beyond data accents.
  • Don't fill pills with light colours.
  • Don't centre more than one message.
  • Don't make the dashboard colourful.
