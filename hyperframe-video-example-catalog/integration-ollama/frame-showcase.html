05

Frame Rules

Pure White · Black Line Art

Do

  • Cut hard from the dark app to the pure white world.
  • Keep white scenes strictly black ink on white with grey subcopy.
  • Let line-art illustrations run edge to edge with grey tyres and speed lines.
  • Use mono command pills and a white terminal card with a green check.
  • End on a heavy wordmark and a single iridescent gem.

Don't

  • Don't add colour, gradients or colour blocks to the white scenes.
  • Don't use real partner mascots or marks without permission.
  • Don't crowd the margins; one headline or one illustration per beat.
  • Don't use a second typeface family for headlines.
