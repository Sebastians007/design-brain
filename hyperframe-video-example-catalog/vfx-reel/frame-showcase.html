05

Frame Rules

One Effect · Calm Middle

Do

  • Hand each effect to the next with a shared shape.
  • Keep one effect per beat; let black and white holds breathe.
  • Keep the paper prompt scene flat and calm.
  • Set captions on a dark translucent card with an active pale-yellow word.
  • Always finish on a pure white logo card.

Don’t

  • Don’t layer two effects in one frame.
  • Don’t use light type on pale grounds.
  • Don’t skip the white end card.
  • Don’t add effects to the paper scene.
