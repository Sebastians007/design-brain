Frame Rules

Calm · Persistent · Rationed

Do

  • Keep one persistent window; show accumulated history plus the composer.
  • Keep the perimeter alive: tiles playing, bars filling.
  • Keep clay inside the window and mint on the product.
  • Use lightness steps and 1px hairlines for depth.
  • Type on at about 0.045s per character with a caret.

Don't

  • Don't crossfade the whole film.
  • Don't overshoot scale beyond 1.04.
  • Don't let clay appear outside the window.
  • Don't add glow on mint or shader transitions.
  • Don't leave a frame static for over a second.
