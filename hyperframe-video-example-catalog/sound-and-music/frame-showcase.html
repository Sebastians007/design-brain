Frame Rules

Paper · Ink · Rationed

Do

  • Keep paper as the stage; terminals and the button are the only dark objects.
  • Use one clay element per frame at most.
  • Click with a quick squash (scale 0.84) and a ripple ring.
  • Enter text waterfalls from the right; exit with a throw left then a hard cut.
  • Set statements in heavy dark sans (600-800).

Don't

  • Don't add a second accent colour.
  • Don't put statements on a dark ground.
  • Don't use more than one clay element per frame.
  • Don't decorate the paper; it stays flat.
