# SITE-BLUEPRINT: ClinicHub multidisciplinary medical clinic

## Source anatomy and identity

SOURCE DECLARED: ClinicHub is officially classified Medical and positioned for clinics, doctors and medical practices. Its home begins with a full-height waiting-room photo, compact floating navigation, rating microcopy, visit action and consultation video panel. Three large scale metrics follow, then a deep-teal clinic-story interval with introductory copy and a photo constellation. The specialty collage surrounds a mint central invitation with six care-area images. The three-step process precedes a family-care benefits composition containing illustrative clinician, calendar and insurance interfaces. Clinician portraits and patient-story panels precede a mint FAQ booking card and a deep-teal closing/footer.

The source uses Lora for hero/section headings and Inter for interface, body, card metadata and illustration labels. Base h1 is50px/60px; base h2 is42px/50.4px. Pale-green panels, white space and deep teal define the live-page design. The aqua background around Marketplace screenshots is presentation artwork. Section positions and heights remain null because source inspection is not rendered geometry.

## Source routes and practical entry points

SOURCE DECLARED: home IDs include expertise, how-it-works, why-us, doctors, reviews and faq. Main visit actions resolve to `/visit-us`; a privacy-policy route is also declared. The inspected `/visit-us` source has a visit-photo hero and request form, location imagery, address/hours, practical questions and shared footer. Its source form fields are full name, email, phone, preferred date and service. Form attributes, source headings, typography presets and layout declarations are saved in secondary-declarations.json and secondary-source-facts.json. The maps and telephone links are destinations, not proof of a real clinic or working office.

OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION: adapt to a small family-care clinic with approved care-area descriptions and a simple inquiry route. Store clinician portraits, names, specialty descriptions and approved biography text separately from marketing metrics. Keep opening hours, address, telephone and inquiry instructions in one reusable record. Use a request workflow with clear owner-approved response expectations and a separately configured provider. If real scheduling is added, distinguish confirmed appointment state from the visitor's requested date.

## Responsive and interaction scope

SOURCE DECLARED: root breakpoints are1200px and680px; reused components additionally expose810px boundaries. Desktop sections use60px horizontal padding, while the hero has an8px inset and15px radius. Source includes Mobile Close navigation, FAQ Closed components, Video/Play, team/review arrows and appear transforms. These identify intended structures; interactions, timing, appointment delivery and availability were not exercised. The benefit calendar and insurance notification are illustrated UI rather than established services.

OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION: collapse service and benefit compositions in normal reading order; retain names, specialties, captions and calls to action. Provide keyboard-operable video/carousel/FAQ controls and reduced-motion static content. Avoid duplicate responsive section IDs in the implementation. All clinician credentials, ratings, satisfaction figures, patient quotations, coverage statements and compliance badges remain sample content until supplied and approved by the actual clinic. This blueprint describes a design reference and supplies no medical advice or practitioner verification.
