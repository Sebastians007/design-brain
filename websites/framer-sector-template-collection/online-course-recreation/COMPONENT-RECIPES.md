## 1. Sketch lesson hero with course progress card

SOURCE DECLARED: PmHero presents “Every skill starts as a sketch” over a full craft photograph. Young Serif h1 declares `clamp(56px,6.6vw,118px)`, line-height `.95`, tracking `-.025em`; supporting Albert Sans copy is 17px/1.55. The progress card uses a 30px/1.04 Young Serif course title, Azeret Mono metadata, school tabs, completion meter and an orange stamp. Desktop copy is absolutely positioned at `left:clamp(24px,3.4vw,56px)` and has `max-width:min(600px,42%)`. The listing describes drawing the sketch into the photograph. OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION: keep the actual headline, photo, meter and actions useful before any pointer interaction; supply a static finished photo for reduced motion and touch. Keep decorative completion percentages separate from an actual student record. Do not require visitors to draw to reveal course information. The dark tactile action can preserve its small orange play chip and inset border without pretending every action launches video.

Repeated desktop/tablet/phone source copies are variants, not extra catalog entries.
