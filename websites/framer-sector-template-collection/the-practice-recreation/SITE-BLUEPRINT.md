SOURCE DECLARED: the practice is officially classified Therapy and explicitly positioned for therapy practices, counselors and psychologists. The inspected `/services/individual-therapy` page contains an introduction and session-fact strip, audience/reasons cards, what-to-expect body, approach cards, questions, repeated consultation form and related services. Its hidden Requested from field records Individual therapy. Other routes are source declarations, not proof that each destination or its backend was exercised.

OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION: adapt the reference to a small therapy collective with owner-approved descriptions for individual, couples, teens/families and online formats. Use service records for audience, practical session facts, overview, approved approach copy and related-service destinations. Keep inquiry configuration shared across home and service pages while retaining the source-route context. Preserve Not sure yet and Just exploring for now selections. Gather only the information the practice actually needs to respond and display accepted-delivery feedback only after the chosen provider acknowledges it.
