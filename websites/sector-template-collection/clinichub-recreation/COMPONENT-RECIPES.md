# ClinicHub component recipes

Evidence: Adheeb Hameed's official Medical Marketplace listing, four official JPGs, home HTML/CSS and the `/visit-us` public source. Source declarations are distinguished from optional implementation recommendations. Appointment requests, availability, practitioner credentials, patient outcomes, ratings, coverage and compliance claims were not verified.

## 1. Photo hero with floating clinic navigation and video

SOURCE DECLARED: Hero - Section is 100vh with an 8px inset and 15px radius. A waiting-room photograph, Black Overlay, Social Proof stars/rating, Lora h1 and primary visit action occupy the left/bottom composition. A separate Video/Play panel and compact caption sit opposite. Navigation is a fixed Nav Frame with Desktop Top Nav and Mobile Close structures; source links target expertise, why-us, doctors, reviews and `/visit-us`. The h1 base is Lora400 50px/60px, becoming40px and32px in source tablet/phone presets. Inter500 18px/21.6px carries the large action, while the video caption is Inter400 14px/19.6px. OPTIONAL: keep the photo, contrast scrim, text and video trigger as separate layers. Give the video button an accessible name and a poster; open a real player only when a video asset is supplied. Show all hero content under reduced motion. Replace ratings and review counts with clinic-approved evidence before publishing.

## 2. Deep teal clinic-story photo constellation

SOURCE DECLARED: About - Section contains an Intro - Section followed by Meet Clinichub- Section. The official imagery shows a centered serif statement surrounded by six small patient/family photos on deep teal. The base h2 is Lora400 42px/50.4px with-.02em tracking; the generic extractor initially found a35px tablet rule, so do not promote that to desktop. Main section frames carry flexible heights; this does not establish rendered y coordinates. OPTIONAL: create a stable photo constellation with explicit positions at the wide layout and a simple grid at compact widths. Keep the central story as normal reading-order text and treat decorative portrait tiles as decorative unless an actual caption exists. Sample assertions about consolidated specialists, waiting times and family care require owner review. Avoid letting photo reveals obscure or delay the clinic introduction.

## 3. Six-specialty collage and illustrated care process

SOURCE DECLARED: `#expertise` combines Dental, Orthopedic, General Medicine, Dermatology, Gynecology and Pediatric Care images around a mint central heading/action card. `#how-it-works` follows with Book Online, Visit For Diagnosis and Ongoing Care. Each step uses a pale-green illustrated panel and a numbered corner label, followed by an Inter500 20px/24px title and Inter400 18px/25.2px paragraph. The expertise section declares desktop padding120px8px80px; process declares80px60px120px. OPTIONAL: model specialty photo cards and process cards as separate components, preserving their different purposes. Specialty tiles can remain descriptive until approved destinations exist. Process order must survive grid collapse. Keep corner labels attached to the correct card and icons away from the title reading order. The three-step text explains a sample journey; it does not demonstrate online scheduling or clinical service delivery.

## 4. Family-care benefits, clinician portraits and patient stories

SOURCE DECLARED: `#why-us` pairs a mother/child image with three translucent benefit cards for doctors, booking and insurance. The nested Booking Form/Calendar/Time/Selected structures belong to UI Illustration. `#doctors` exposes portraits, names, specialty labels and left/right controls. `#reviews` exposes desktop Variant1 and Mobile1 testimonial layouts with a large photo beside a mint quote/percentage panel. Multiple reviews IDs belong to separate responsive variants in the source. OPTIONAL: retain the miniature calendar as a decorative illustration unless a scheduler is separately configured. Use real clinician data and approved portraits for directory records, and approved quotes for stories. Build carousel controls as labelled buttons with a static card-list alternative; keep quotes and associated names together. Publish only one active anchor per section. Demo rating, satisfaction and coverage statements are sample content rather than verified outcomes.

## 5. FAQ invitation and secondary visit request

SOURCE DECLARED: `#faq` places a mint booking card next to Closed FAQ question components concerning walk-ins, family bookings, same-day appointments, insurance and consultation duration. The separate `/visit-us` page uses a photo hero with Text & Form, followed by Our Location - Section, address/hours and FAQ, then the common footer. Its form declares required Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Preferred Date and Select Service fields. Source Phone Number is type number; service options cover general checkup, dental, pediatric, gynecology, orthopedics, dermatology and other. OPTIONAL: retain the visual compact request form but use a telephone-appropriate field when implementing an actual contact workflow. Associate labels and errors explicitly, explain that a preferred date is a request, and show confirmation only after the configured provider acknowledges delivery. Render FAQ answers through keyboard-operable controls. The footer's ISO/HIPAA badge images are unverified demonstration assets; replacement or removal is an owner content task.
