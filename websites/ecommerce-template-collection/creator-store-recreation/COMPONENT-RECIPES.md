# Creator Store — five component recipes

Evidence: homepage HTML/CSS and five official images. Dimensions are declarations or recommendations, not live geometry. Custom components carry the design; ’s generic Inter preset is insufficient.

## 1. The dimensional catalogue shelf

Build `.dsh` as a walnut, viewport-height room. Use a 0.9:1.1 desktop grid: generous cream/orange headline and receipt at left, shelf scene at right. Declare 1500px perspective with its origin near the top; rotate the 620px shelf unit 7 degrees on X and −16 degrees on Y. Three 206px rows separated by 34px hold nine packages. Each package needs six faces driven by independent width, height and depth tokens. Vary books, tins, folders, kits and boxes. Apply tinted covers, barcodes, monospaced badges and contact shadows. Hover/focus lifts an object forward and reveals its price tag. At phone width, use the declared front-cover scroll-snap rail with 176px minimum objects and permanently exposed price/Add information.

## 2. The pointed price-tag action

Compose `.ds-btn` from a clipped `.ds-tag`, punched hole, fill layer, label window and square arrow/bag chip. The polygon has a 16px left point; minimum action height is 50px, left text inset 30px and chip size 34px. Primary tags use orange with dark ink; quiet variants use cream or translucent light against walnut. Hover/focus expands a circular fill from the hole and briefly swings the tag around its left anchor. Duplicate labels translate through an overflow window. Keep Manrope 14px/700 action text readable and retain the explicit focus outline. Mobile labels wrap naturally and remove the duplicate animation line. Magnetic offsets are declared variables; runtime pointer tracking remains unverified.

## 3. The paper receipt

Use cream paper against dark walnut, narrow Azeret Mono labels, dashed separators, aligned currency columns and a perforated zigzag lower edge. The hero receipt is at most 420px wide, padded 16/18/22px and rotated −1.4 degrees; phone variant removes rotation. Include three compact order rows, a restrained sales-summary line and an angled stamp. Reuse this material for bundle totals and reviews: emphasize totals; give review prose comfortable space. Treat statistics and verification labels as sample content until connected to real orders.

## 4. The bundle stack and arithmetic panel

Create three large coloured boards for navy, violet and green bundles. Desktop `.dsb-in` splits 1.25:0.75, with a 24px radius, roughly 440px minimum height and 24–44px padding. The left side groups title, description, product thumbnails and a tag action; the right carries a cream receipt with line items, subtotal, bundle price and saving. Sticky stack structures are declared; choreography needs implementation and verification. Phone and tablet boards collapse to one column; phone uses 20px padding and an 18px radius. Validate structured price arithmetic before displaying savings.

## 5. The aisle rows and section rhythm

On cream, build six numbered full-width category links with fine rules, oversized Gabarito names, monospaced product counts, short Manrope descriptions and a small opening affordance. Desktop rows use four columns: 64px index, name, description and action. Phone reduces the name to 24px and reflows details within a three-column structure. Follow the actual homepage sequence through featured window, contents, bundles, syllabus, numbers, reviews, studio, free guide, FAQ, notes and closing CTA. Preserve diagonal section edges, dark reviews and closing CTA, then footer columns, newsletter and faint wordmark. Section headers have an explicit 400-important uppercase rule; the hero remains heavier and sentence case.
