# Ecommerce recreation kits

Open collection-showcase.html to compare five storefronts across four Ecommerce categories. Choose one folder or ZIP and paste its AI-HANDOFF.md into the building AI. Each retains FRAME.md, frame-showcase.html, reference-frames.jpg and caption-skin.html, plus fonts/media, detailed source maps, recipes and commerce guidance. Read CATEGORY-GUIDE.md before choosing.

Public home/catalog/product source and official artwork were inspected; Creator Store checkout source was inspected without executing a purchase. Layout/motion and shopping backend behavior remain unverified.
