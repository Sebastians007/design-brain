# Attest — legal services blueprint

This recreation helps a prospective business client decide whether the firm handles their concern, whether its people seem approachable, what evidence supports trust, and how to begin a conversation. It supplies a presentation system, not legal advice or verified legal results.

## Decision journey

SOURCE DECLARED: The home sequence is hero with logos, mission, services, testimonials, team, contact CTA, support/FAQs, and footer. Navigation links reach about, reviews, team, and contact anchors; footer links include five service detail routes. Booking actions point to an external calendar, while a contact email uses a mailto link. The source establishes destinations and component structures; it does not establish successful appointments, email delivery, or a working backend.

OPTIONAL: Preserve the short path from practice fit to human contact. Use the mission tiles to explain the working relationship, the service selector to narrow the visitor’s need, and attorney profiles to identify relevant expertise. Let users contact the firm without consuming every proof item.

## CMS model

SOURCE DECLARED: Named service routes cover business setup, contracts, employment, disputes, and trademarks/IP. Team cards have names, roles, portraits, and LinkedIn destinations. Testimonial structures contain quote, author, affiliation, and stars. These visible records do not prove a CMS exists.

OPTIONAL: Define PracticeArea with slug, title, introduction, scope, related attorneys, approved FAQ references, image, and contact destination. Define Attorney with portrait, role, biography, practice relationships, jurisdiction, verified admission details, and public profile links. Define Proof with type, attributed text, source, permission status, approval date, and display eligibility. If an approved result is eventually supplied, store its context and supporting evidence separately; do not generate success metrics or case outcomes. Keep editorial approval fields private.

## Contact and publication states

SOURCE DECLARED: The page offers scheduling, email, and FAQ entry points. It declares accordion Open/Closed variants and device-specific navigation structures. Runtime transitions remain unverified.

OPTIONAL: Design contact states for idle, focused, invalid, submitting, success, and failure only if a form is added. Clearly describe what a successful submission means using the actual implementation. For external scheduling, show a normal link and recoverable unavailable state. Add accessible focus, reduced motion, meaningful portrait alternatives, and approved privacy/contact language. Gate publishing on firm review of credentials, testimonials, service promises, and destinations; keep administrative approval tooling out of the public interface.
