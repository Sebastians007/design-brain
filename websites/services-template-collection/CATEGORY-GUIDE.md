# Services: five design references

This batch focuses on Services, the umbrella that includes Consulting, Education, Home and Legal. Two consulting references provide different art directions; the other three broaden the business patterns. This is a reference collection, not a working service business or a claim that booking/contact infrastructure has been connected.

| Template | Branch | Visual direction | Useful pattern | Visitor's main decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kora | Consulting | Warm, growth-oriented presentation, variable sans typography and green accents | Service narratives, before/after framing, case evidence and package cards | Is this engagement relevant to our growth problem? |
| Westbridge | Consulting | Restrained dark editorial typography and professional portrait media | Expertise, people, service routes and case-story hierarchy | Can I trust this team with a consequential business decision? |
| Bright Path | Education | Cream, orange and green with playful display type and learning portraits | Programs, levels, mentors, process and session offers | Does this program fit the learner and can I try a session? |
| Solshine | Home services | Cinematic solar imagery, black/white stages and lime directional accents | Installation capabilities, process, projects and quote-oriented conversion | Is this provider a good fit for the property's needs? |
| Attest | Legal | Warm serif/sans hierarchy, cream surfaces and a dark brown proof band | Practice areas, human attorney profiles and consultation invitations | Does this firm handle my issue and is it approachable? |

## What these references have in common

Service visitors need to identify an offer, find evidence, understand the process and take a useful next step. The relative emphasis changes by business. A tutoring page needs program and learner fit; a solar page needs property/project relevance; a consulting page needs engagement fit and proof; a legal page needs practice fit and a clear human contact path. Preserve these decision structures without turning all five designs into one common layout.

## What is included

Every template retains the original FRAME.md, frame-showcase.html, reference-frames.jpg and caption-skin.html format. Source assets, source declaration maps, tokens, an AI prompt, component recipes, routes/CMS notes, responsive/motion guidance and a fidelity checklist accompany those four files. SERVICE-BLUEPRINT.md distinguishes source-declared conversion/content patterns from optional implementation recommendations.

## Recommended implementation additions

These are recommendations, not claims about native preview behavior. Provide a meaningful submit/loading/success/error state for inquiries; keep labels and keyboard focus visible; use clear destination links for services and people; reserve photographic aspect ratios while assets load; preserve a readable static version of reveals and tickers. Configure a real booking or message-delivery destination only when one is supplied and authorized. For a faithful recreation, do not add visible forms, calculators, guarantees or extra sections merely because a blueprint mentions a possible extension.

Store service offers, program or practice records, people, proof records, testimonials and articles as structured data where that source uses them. Keep dates, destinations, media focal points and attribution alongside each record. Source demo prices, figures and testimonials are reference content; they do not establish a real business's fees or results. Hidden breakpoint and hover duplicates should resolve to one canonical record.

## How to use the collection

Choose one folder or individual ZIP. Paste that folder's AI-HANDOFF.md into the building AI, and have it inspect the fonts, asset map, source declarations, image sheet and recipes before implementing. Judge the hero, complete section sequence, typography, image crop and footer first, then compare phone/tablet layouts and visible control states. Document substitutions and unverified dimensions.

Evidence coverage is public home-preview HTML/CSS and official marketplace artwork. The twelve crops per template are traceable image details, not twelve independent browser captures. Device frames, outer color mats and presentation shadows are promotional. Native rendered geometry, playback timing and server-side booking/contact behavior were not tested. Secondary pages are declared routes unless explicitly documented otherwise.
