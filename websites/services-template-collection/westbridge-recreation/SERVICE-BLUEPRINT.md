# Westbridge service-site blueprint

**Visitor decision — interpretation:** The page helps a founder, executive or investor decide whether the firm suits a consequential business problem, assess its operating approach and initiate contact. The portrait establishes a human presence; expertise narrows fit; approach and outcomes support credibility; insights offer a lower-commitment next step. This describes the information architecture, not measured conversion behavior.

**SOURCE DECLARED offers:** Homepage service links point to M&A Advisory, Performance and Growth detail routes. Their short descriptors concern transactions/integration, operating scale and market expansion. Contact and partner actions point to `/contact`; company information, case studies and insights have separate routes. The home sequence is hero, welcome, expertise, approach, testimonial, insights and footer. The case-studies route is reached through a proof action rather than a home case-study grid.

**SOURCE DECLARED proof:** The welcome area supplies four scale figures. Approach panels pair operating principles with metrics and imagery. The testimonial area includes an attributed executive, company mark, three outcomes and a Play affordance. These are template claims, not independently substantiated results. No captured video element verifies playback. **OPTIONAL recommendation:** Replace demonstration claims with approved evidence, give each result its engagement context and timeframe, and connect testimonial playback only when a licensed recording exists.

**CMS schema — OPTIONAL recommendation:** Define `Service` with title, slug, short descriptor, hero image, problem, engagement scope and related studies; `CaseStudy` with client, sector, challenge, approach, approved outcomes and attribution; `Insight` with title, slug, category, publication date, cover, excerpt and body; `Testimonial` with quote, person, role, company mark and optional media reference. Add image alt text and evidence approval fields. Routes and repeated card fields make these entities useful, but public HTML does not establish their runtime CMS implementation.

**Contact states:** SOURCE DECLARED home CTAs navigate to contact. The footer exposes a required email subscription input, submit button and hidden anti-spam-style fields; it does not reveal a verified delivery service or response flow. **OPTIONAL recommendation:** Implement distinct idle, invalid, submitting, success and retryable-error states, with persistent labels and announced feedback. A contact form may collect name, business email, company, service interest and a concise challenge; those fields are proposed, not observed. Preserve entered information on failure and supply a verified alternative contact route.
