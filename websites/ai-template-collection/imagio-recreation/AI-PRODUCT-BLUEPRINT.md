# Imagio — generative image product blueprint

**SOURCE DECLARED.** The official listing positions this template for AI image and content-generation products. The home preview contains an image-led composer hero, a process explanation, capability cards, output imagery, audience solutions, plans, testimonials, articles, FAQ and a closing invitation/footer. A public GET of `/features` was saved separately; it declares a creative-workflow presentation with prompt-assistant, generation and export tabs, a product illustration, audience-style panels, additional proof content and the shared navigation/footer. Those structures establish marketing intent, not an operational generation backend.

**OPTIONAL RECREATION: product premise.** Adapt the system for a small visual-concept tool serving independent shops and content teams. Use an original proposition about exploring campaign artwork, then show one concrete input-to-output example: a short description of a ceramic object on a pale studio surface, three stylistic variations, and a selected square crop. Keep the pastel output palette connected to the warm website without making every result botanical.

**OPTIONAL RECREATION: demo flow.** Let visitors choose a curated sample brief, adjust a style chip and view a prepared example set. Label this a sample preview beside the action. Use empty, editing, loading, results, no-results and retry states. Preserve the visitor’s text after a failed attempt. A result tile opens an accessible detail view containing its example description, style, aspect ratio and image alternative text. Provide an explicit return action. Add export or live generation only when the real product supports it.

**OPTIONAL RECREATION: content plan.** Explain the workflow with three brief task statements. Give each capability a visible output consequence rather than a sweeping promise. Pair audience stories with credible examples for product imagery, campaign concepts and editorial illustrations. Populate pricing from actual limits and billing rules; source amounts are demo copy. Replace invented testimonials and metrics with genuine proof. Use two articles that teach visual briefing and composition. Write FAQ answers for the actual product’s formats, ownership terms and usage limits.

**OPTIONAL RECREATION: release checks.** Confirm literal font families and nested overrides, maintain cream/brown contrast and verify the composer at each declared root breakpoint. Check keyboard operation of menus, chips, comparison controls and disclosures. Prefer a stable fan composition when reduced motion is requested. Keep decorative images out of reading order and ensure all results have meaningful descriptions. Treat business claims, rights, quotas and service speed as content requiring product evidence.
