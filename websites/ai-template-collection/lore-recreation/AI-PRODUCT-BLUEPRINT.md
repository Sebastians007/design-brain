# Lore — generative video product blueprint

**SOURCE DECLARED.** The marketplace describes a template for video, image and audio generation platforms. The public home order is Hero, process, Companies, Showcase, Features, Dashboard and News, followed by a standalone CTA and Footer. Its hero and showcase declare MP4 files with posters. A separate public GET of `/product` was inspected: it contains a product introduction, a workspace visual with chat-style creation controls, capability content and shared closing/navigation structures. These are presentation facts. The source does not establish model integrations, generated output, export reliability or actual collaboration.

**OPTIONAL RECREATION: product premise.** Adapt Lore for a short-scene concepting workspace used by small film and campaign teams. Lead with one original story seed and three contrasting treatments. Use an atmospheric poster that stays legible under the left title. Keep the dark canvas and white action hierarchy, while allowing output imagery to carry the expressive color. Describe the real product’s supported duration, formats and editing scope plainly.

**OPTIONAL RECREATION: sample interaction.** Offer a prepared scene sequence rather than implying an unconnected service creates video. Visitors select a story seed, choose a treatment and open a curated result. Identify the experience as a sample preview at the point of action. A result detail includes a poster, optional controlled clip, scene description and supported metadata. Define idle, selected, preview-loading, preview-ready, paused, unavailable-media and retry states. Keep the selected treatment after failure. Announce status separately from decorative progress illustrations and support reduced motion with posters.

**OPTIONAL RECREATION: page content.** The process should explain brief, scene exploration and review in concise task language. Showcase a small set of visibly different examples with original captions. Replace model counts, resolution claims and unrestricted-usage language with supported product facts. Use the dashboard to demonstrate how a saved project is organized. Let news entries document real improvements and practical creative guidance. Populate pricing on the actual route from current billing details; home presentation does not prove plan behavior. Link the closing action to a real onboarding destination.

**OPTIONAL RECREATION: implementation checks.** Preserve DM Sans roles, Lily Script One branding and nested muted heading spans. Inspect media readability at the declared 810px and 1200px anchors. Check keyboard and touch operation of menus, carousel controls, prompt details and player controls. Keep active media count low, retain posters on failures and make focus visible against black surfaces. Validate claims and sample labeling before release; avoid shipping the template’s account placeholder or purchase badge as product UI.
