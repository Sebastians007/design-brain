# SecureAI — enterprise AI blueprint

SecureAI is an explicitly relevant reference for AI security, observability, governance, and enterprise deployment messaging. Its public homepage combines a theatrical mineral hero with operational demonstrations, integrations, architecture capabilities, platform metrics, pricing, and editorial links. This blueprint separates those declared presentation structures from optional product requirements.

## Declared page and route structure

Retain the homepage sequence recorded in the config: navigation; Header; Platform; Core Capabilities; Integrations; Expertise; Platform Insights; Pricing; Articles; creator custom-development promotion; Footer. The four capability areas concern agent activity, response, security rules, and visibility. Enterprise pricing names access reviews, audit logs, policy checks, and incident alerts. These are demo content, not proof of available services. The public Services route was inspected by GET and introduces capability, platform, expertise, certification, agent-security, pricing, and article structures. Source typography and media conditions are preserved in its secondary declaration file without text samples.

## Optional product states

For a working enterprise adaptation, define an environment selector and a trace identifier before any alert view. A trace can be empty, queued, evaluating, awaiting human review, allowed, blocked, or errored. Show policy version, responsible team, reason, timestamp, and data sensitivity where appropriate. A blocked action should offer investigation and safe retry paths; retry must disclose what will be rerun. Review controls need explicit confirmation of the chosen permission scope. These states are recommendations, not interactions verified in the template.

## Content, trust, and deployment

Replace demonstration counters and plan copy with approved product information. Publish deployment choices only when supported: hosted, customer cloud, private network, region restrictions, and disconnected operation need separate definitions. State what crosses the provider boundary and what retention applies to prompts, responses, metadata, and audit records. Link claims to dated evidence, named controls, and requestable documentation. The source's Services certification section establishes a presentation opportunity; it does not establish certification status.

## Presentation handoff

Keep Chakra Petch for the hero and operational labels, Jakarta for body and broad section headlines, and full normal/italic declarations in the implementation. Use the 810px and 1200px principal boundaries as source anchors. All motion remains optional until tested: public markup only exposes initial transforms and named animation components. A reduced-motion version should reveal complete text immediately and preserve the same content order. The result should explain security decisions without requiring visitors to interpret decorative motion.
