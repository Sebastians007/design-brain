# SecureAI — five component recipes

These authored instructions use public homepage declarations and official imagery. Typography is declared; layout recommendations are unmeasured. Product behavior was not tested.

## 1. Mineral hero and bracket frame

Build a black hero with a left text column and a larger overlapping image field. Use four corner brackets, diagonal hatching, the mineral cutout, and a small lower-right proposition. The rock interrupts the frame while hatching remains visible behind it. Use Chakra Petch 700 for the uppercase headline; its desktop declaration is 68px with a 90% line height and negative tracking. The eyebrow is smaller, cyan, and widely tracked. Place Plus Jakarta Sans explanation below. On phones, place text first and crop the mineral around its cyan seam. Keep atmospheric furniture out of the meaningful reading order.

## 2. Workflow console and review queue

Compose the Platform demonstration as a console beside two narrow progress groups. Separate header, entry area, explanation, and trailing action. Distinguish progress from review through labels and colored surfaces; each card needs an identifier, action title, and status. Use angular Chakra Petch for console labels, and Jakarta for annotations. Recommend 24px internal gaps and thin strokes. Optional states are queued, processing, review-required, resolved, and failed. Interactive controls should explain changes in a textual log and require an explicit action before applying a policy.

## 3. Capability and expertise card families

Keep two card families. Core Capabilities introduces four compact concepts with line graphics and short text. Expertise gives architecture, integration, memory, and operations substantial illustrated cards in two columns. Shared icon sizes and label positions connect them without identical heights or backgrounds. Preserve expansive Jakarta section headings and tighter Chakra card labels. Recommend square corners, 1px rules, and restrained cyan/lime details. On narrow screens, order cards by semantic category. Decorative stroke reveals should finish fully readable; essential differences need text rather than movement or hue alone.

## 4. Asymmetric platform-insight panels

Create a two-by-two composition whose upper and lower panels use different visual evidence: mineral imagery, large numeric counters, repository statistics, execution information, and bar-like throughput graphics. Keep each panel's concept, explanation, and metric together. Labels must say whether values are illustrative, measured, estimated, or unavailable. For an authored product adaptation, add time range, environment, and last-update metadata rather than carrying the demo's enormous numbers into a launch page. A blank panel should explain the required connection; a loading panel should retain its title. Use real text for values so resizing and assistive reading work independently of the graphics.

## 5. Enterprise plan and evidence exit

Build three pricing cards, with the Enterprise card directing visitors toward a sales conversation. Group operational features into readable rows and distinguish included features from add-ons or planned capabilities. Keep article links immediately after pricing, then retain the creator promotion if reproducing the reference faithfully. A product adaptation can replace that promotion with approved procurement material. Footer legal labels need real destinations: the reference currently points those labels to a creator profile. Recommend visible focus, clear contact wording, and an evidence link near enterprise claims. Certification or deployment assurances require product-owned substantiation; decorative shields, log samples, and template labels do not supply it.
