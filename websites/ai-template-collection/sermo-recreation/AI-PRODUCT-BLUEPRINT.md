# Sermo voice-product blueprint

## Product promise and demonstration

SOURCE DECLARED: Sermo is a dark conversational-voice reference with a command-console hero, a live-call indicator, support statistics, agent composition, learning insights and a dedicated Voice Layer scene. The visual story follows an operator from setup to ongoing improvement. Its claims and customer figures are template content, not independently verified results. Source declarations and official imagery establish structure; they do not establish audio playback, transcription quality or backend automation.

OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION: adapt it for a support voice agent that answers common billing questions and hands complex cases to a person. The demonstration should include a short customer question, a policy-grounded response and a handoff summary. Offer a safe prerecorded sample before inviting any live call. Make the mode and sample duration visible, and give users an always-available transcript. Keep voice identity consistent across the hero console and later product card.

## Reading order and secondary route

SOURCE DECLARED: preserve navigation → hero → logo strip → statistics → support complexity → Composer → Improve → Smart Insights → Voice → Voice Card → integrations → customer spotlight → CTA → footer. Improve and Smart Insights, and Voice and Voice Card, are separate source frames. There is no home pricing section. The declared Documentation route was fetched and its heading/type/media declarations saved; it presents quickstart-oriented support content within the same dark product language.

OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION: place implementation requirements in documentation, including phone routing, knowledge sources, review policies and transfer behavior. Give each home module one meaningful example rather than a long capability list. Explain how a policy change is proposed, reviewed and applied. Replace template business metrics with measured evidence, including period and denominator. Make integrations discoverable through an explicit connector list, with documentation for setup and permissions.

## Interaction contract

SOURCE DECLARED: menu drawer, console modes, composer steps, waveform structures and a paused voice-card state exist. Animation timing, waveform synchronization and operational state transitions remain untested.

OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION: specify idle, loading, playing, paused, finished and unavailable media states. Console suggestions should announce content updates without stealing focus. A transfer state should show the retained context and the next human action. When a sample fails, preserve its transcript. Supply reduced-motion and static-spectrum fallbacks, and prevent decorative rays from obscuring labels. On phone, stack explanations before their product scenes, retain the complete narrative order and constrain the oversized footer wordmark. Keep links to product, integrations and documentation functional before launching the adaptation.
