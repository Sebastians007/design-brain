# Create component recipes

These original recipes interpret Create by Tamas Bodo at https://createstudio.framer.media/. Public HTML/CSS supplies structure and typography; four official promotional images supply visual references. Their monitors, surrounding flowers, and phone mockups are marketplace framing. Sizes below are recommendations unless explicitly identified as source declarations. Runtime interaction was not observed.

## 1. Portrait stage

Compose the hero on a dark full-width stage, with the blue floral portrait centered behind the content. Crop around the flower crown and visible face; keep the mouth below the main identity so the image retains a human anchor. Place indexed mono marks on a narrow left rail, a compact proposition above the oversized Figtree identity, and a trust block toward the upper right. Supporting copy, location/time, and two actions occupy the lower left; the showreel sits opposite. Reading order should follow that hierarchy even when visual columns diverge. Recommend click-to-play media, a still fallback, and restrained entrance transforms.

## 2. CMS project presentation

Make each project a complete content unit rather than a generic image tile. The source supplies project name, discipline, year, image, and a case-study route for each entry. Use a large Figtree project title, with quieter mono metadata and thin rules establishing separate rails. Keep brand artwork dominant and preserve logos through contain sizing where required; crop photographic media only within its own frame. Read name and discipline before imagery, then year and destination. The desktop title declaration is 4 root units at weight 500. Recommend one clear card link, visible keyboard focus, and no essential information hidden behind a custom cursor.

## 3. Benefit evidence grid

Build the light benefit section with a narrow section tag, broad headline, supporting sentence, then a deliberately varied grid. The source declares nine desktop columns, six tablet columns, and one phone column; individual spans should accommodate each card’s content. Combine a short process statement, satisfaction statistic with compact feature bars, custom-work illustration, plan preview, and availability media. Keep coral for the decisive number or action, leaving most surfaces pale. Within each card, read heading, evidence or graphic, then explanatory caption. Recommend independent static fallbacks for decorative phases and manual carousel controls; preserve the information when motion is disabled.

## 4. Leadership portrait strip

Use four grayscale portraits on the dark team stage, with generous separation from the section introduction. The official image suggests subtle perspective variation rather than four identical corporate rectangles. Crop close enough to retain the unusual expressions and costumes, while reserving a quiet lower edge for name and role. A KPI belongs near the upper corner and must remain legible over the photograph. Keep the source’s person-by-person reading order: portrait identity, role, evidence, social destinations. Recommend flattening perspective on narrow screens and revealing expanded information on focus or tap. Hover may soften the image, but must never conceal the person’s name.

## 5. Closing invitation stage

Place the final call invitation over soft pale abstract background media. Split tenure and delivery evidence into the left column, with the larger invitation, founder portrait, attributed statement, and coral action on the right. Crop the background freely because it is atmospheric; contain the portrait and avoid overlaying fine copy on strong background intersections. Read the evidence before the invitation, then the supporting voice and action. Source CSS declares a sticky 100vh desktop stage and natural phone height. Recommend ordinary document flow on phones, a calm still fallback for the background video, and a direct contact destination with a clear accessible name.
