# Ten AI Framer references: two designs for each product type

This collection adds two references each for Chatbot/support, Voice/meetings, Generative media, Developer/API and Enterprise/trust. These are practical product archetypes inside AI/technology, not an assertion that Framer uses exactly these category labels. Selection fit is grounded in official descriptions and source page content; the scope of each kit is recorded individually.

Tessera Dev is specifically a developer infrastructure reference; its selection does not imply a working AI inference service. Plinth fits both developer infrastructure and enterprise trust, but is assigned once to the enterprise pair. The two voice selections are genuinely voice-agent-oriented, rather than meeting tools chosen solely by name. Marketing claims and simulated product scenes are not validated services.

## Evidence and quality upgrade

Every home preview was opened and captured at1363×936 in the live browser. The native opening image, computed heading styles, named frame boxes and observed page height are in each kit. Four additional captures record Voxa pricing after a label action, Imagio after a menu action, Tessera after Data tab selection, and a scrolled Plinth product scene. LIVE-PREVIEW-REVIEW.md describes what actually happened and where a completed control transition was not confirmed. Browser images are separate from the twelve official-artwork crops per template.

This viewport does not prove the desktop variant for every source: Chatassist has a1440px upper anchor and showed a hamburger header at1363px; Plinth also declares a1440px boundary. Phone and tablet views were not rendered. CSS alternatives and responsive recommendations are preserved, with actual principal anchors per source. Exact motion timing, audio/model execution, forms, authentication, APIs, payments, certifications and real integrations remain untested.

## AI handoff

Choose one whole template folder or its individual ZIP. Paste AI-HANDOFF.md into the building AI. Have it inspect FRAME.md, COMPONENT-RECIPES.md, source-declarations.json, assets-manifest.json, AI-PRODUCT-BLUEPRINT.md, secondary-declarations.json, DEMO-STATES.json and LIVE-PREVIEW-REVIEW.md. Open screenshots/live-home.jpg, the field guide and reference sheet before implementation. Native heading and frame measurements should be used only for their recorded viewport/state; nested spans and variable-font axes still need source resolution.

Build the complete page order in the existing stack. Preserve image focal crops, atmosphere, mixed font roles, whitespace and repeated product UI shapes. Use local assets and deterministic fixtures for illustrative activity. Do not invent extra forms, chat inputs, permission dialogs, security badges, generation features or checkout flows because the optional blueprint mentions a possible state. A reference should remain useful even without a backend.

Render at source-specific desktop/tablet/phone widths, test keyboard and reduced-motion access, and compare the implementation at1363×936 to the captured opening. Test connected products separately when authorized services and endpoints are provided. Record substitutions and unobserved states; never claim complete visual parity or real product capability based solely on source declarations or these snapshots.
