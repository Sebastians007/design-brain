# Bright Path — five component recipes

Basis: supplied public HTML/CSS and four official images. Exclude orange mats and green collage surrounds used in promotion. Distinguish source declarations from implementation recommendations.

## 1. Learner-centered hero

SOURCE DECLARED: the hero combines a pastel eyebrow, separately wrapped display words, a green highlight, decorative strokes, two button links, rounded learner photographs, and repeated SVG text on a curved ribbon. Its desktop h1 uses Bricolage Grotesque 700 at 103px, with 103px line height and −0.06em tracking; CSS reduces it to 62px and 50px. Primary intent routes to contact, while exploration routes to programs. RECOMMENDATION: preserve the focal learner photograph and enough negative space for the headline. Keep illustration noninteractive, hide duplicate ribbon wording from assistive technology, and supply a static reduced-motion treatment. Actual ribbon animation was not verified.

## 2. Pastel program card

SOURCE DECLARED: four subject cards carry an icon, subject title, brief description, and rounded photograph; each links to a specific program detail route. Blue, lime, pink, and peach differentiate subjects without becoming navigation rules. Titles declare Bricolage Grotesque 500, 24px/28.8px, with −0.04em tracking. RECOMMENDATION: make one accessible card link, retain meaningful image alternatives, and keep descriptions similar in length. Use a recommended 20px radius and collapse the four-column desktop arrangement at narrow widths. Treat the collection model as an implementation proposal rather than a verified CMS.

## 3. Mentor portrait card

SOURCE DECLARED: four linked mentor cards show name, teaching subject, portrait, and a short human introduction. Names declare Bricolage Grotesque 500, 22px/26.4px, −0.04em tracking. The actual home Team section has a blue CSS background, although other promotional views show cream surrounds. Individual mentor routes and the index route are evidenced. RECOMMENDATION: preserve portrait consistency, subject labeling, and text legibility across differently colored cards. Store biography and program associations separately from presentation. An optional credential field can strengthen parent confidence after verification; supplied template descriptions do not establish real staff qualifications or availability.

## 4. Monthly plan comparison

SOURCE DECLARED: the home pricing chapter offers Group, Private, and Intensive plans, with monthly figures of $149, $289, and $449. Private has a popular variant. Cards separate price, cadence, summary, feature rows, and an inclusion link to the pricing route. RECOMMENDATION: align comparison rows and emphasize the billing unit as clearly as the number. Keep the chosen-plan affordance a pricing/inquiry link unless a payment service is deliberately added. Mobile cards should follow the same comparison order. The displayed amounts are template content, not validated commercial terms. Current fees, eligibility, pauses, and session frequency need owner-approved content before launch.

## 5. Reassurance accordion and inquiry handoff

SOURCE DECLARED: five FAQ items have question, divider, and answer structures plus open/closed desktop and phone variants. A large final free-session panel then provides contact links, followed by a dark footer with newsletter-form markup and page/legal links. Official contact imagery shows name, email, program choices, and learner context. RECOMMENDATION: implement FAQ controls as buttons with expanded state and visible focus, retain a helpful first answer, and give inquiry submission clear pending, success, and error states. Do not assume the photographed contact form or newsletter markup proves delivery. Connect the form only to an explicitly configured destination, with a concise response expectation.
