# Bright Path — education services blueprint

Bright Path, credited to Salim from Webestica, is an Education example within Services. The supplied preview and official imagery describe a tutoring decision: a parent or student needs to identify the subject, starting level, teaching format, suitable mentor, and affordable next step. Cream surfaces, forest typography, pastel cards, rounded portraits, and handwritten emphasis make this decision approachable.

## SOURCE DECLARED

The homepage progresses from hero to programs, evidence metrics, process, mentors, learner levels, in-center/online formats, family stories, pricing, FAQs, final inquiry CTA, and footer. Public links declare program/mentor indexes and detail routes, pricing, contact, about, and legal pages. Free-session and schedule actions lead to contact. The source describes three monthly plans and a first-session assessment process. These are template claims; the available evidence does not verify enrollment, payment, scheduling, a learner dashboard, form delivery, or a persistent CMS.

Official contact imagery shows name and email fields, program selection, and learner context. It also presents a staged follow-up explanation. Preserve this as an inquiry experience until an operational booking destination is configured. Footer newsletter markup is a separate subscription structure and should not imply a working subscriber database.

## OPTIONAL implementation recommendations

Use a Programs collection with title, slug, subject icon, accent color, short introduction, image/alt text, eligible ages, levels, curriculum blocks, session duration, weekly frequency, formats, linked mentors, related FAQs, and inquiry CTA. Use a Mentors collection with name, slug, portrait/alt text, subject references, introduction, full biography, verified credentials, specialties, teaching formats, and contact CTA. These schemas are proposed; route evidence alone does not prove their storage model.

Let families compare programs first, then review mentor suitability and practical commitments before sending an inquiry. Pass the chosen subject and plan into the form as editable context. Ask only for information needed to make the initial match. Provide confirmation, a response timeframe, and a recovery path after errors; add calendar scheduling only when availability is maintained.

Present price, billing cadence, session count, group size, and cancellation terms together. Validate template performance claims and testimonial consent before publication. Reconcile the source’s differing review totals across metric and testimonial sections. Link trust evidence to its origin where available, and replace placeholder contact/social destinations with owner-approved details.
