# Kora services blueprint

Reference: Joseph Alexander’s Kora consulting preview at https://kora.framer.media/. This plan separates source-declared interface content from optional implementation recommendations. Evidence covers public HTML/CSS and promotional screenshots; backend and interactions remain unverified.

## SOURCE DECLARED

The visitor’s first decision is whether to inspect services, compare pricing, or book a conversation. A flower-image hero presents this choice alongside trust avatars, partner artwork, and a case teaser. Problem and before/after comparison establish the rationale for outside help. Services then supply a growth-comparison graphic and five numbered offers: Go-to-Market, Growth Strategy, Revenue Operations, Sales Optimization, and Pricing & Packaging. Each combines a benefit title, subservices, contact route, photograph, and quote.

The process sequence is Diagnose → Design → Build → Transfer, followed by a demo preview and benefits. Team profiles introduce the people before the testimonial block, results grid, founder invitation, and detailed Sitemark case. These are authored examples of proof, not independently validated business outcomes. Pricing presents Growth Sprint, Growth Partnership, and Custom selectors, with an initially supplied price/benefit panel. FAQ and articles precede the final contact invitation.

Public routes support cases and insights archives, individual case/article pages, about, contact, privacy, and terms. Home article cards expose category, date, author, image, and detail links; the featured case exposes metadata, service tags, stats, quote, and artwork. Repeated structures suggest CMS suitability; collections and editorial permissions remain unverified.

The contact interface declares required name/email fields, service selections, revenue qualification, challenge text, agreement, and a submit/loading structure. The footer adds newsletter signup, address/contact details, social/legal links, a founder quote, and oversized wordmark. Clipboard and mobile-menu state structures exist; their outcomes remain untested.

## OPTIONAL IMPLEMENTATION

Model services, team members, reviews, cases, articles, and FAQs as separate content types; keep proof claims attached to approval evidence. Preserve the home reading sequence and give every service a stable anchor. Bind pricing selection to explicit scope, price, and benefits with clear selected-state semantics.

Implement form validation, submitting, confirmed success, and recoverable failure as distinct states. Retain entries on failure and report delivery only after an actual endpoint confirms it. Configure booking, newsletter delivery, article publishing, and consent handling explicitly. These integrations are recommendations, not capabilities established by the source preview.
