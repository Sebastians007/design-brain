# Solshine — five component recipes

These recipes interpret source declarations and official images. Recommended dimensions are starting values, not measurements. Diagonal backgrounds and floating frames are promotional framing; omit them from the site.

## 1. Photographic energy hero

**SOURCE DECLARED:** The hero uses a solar photograph, dark overlay, bottom-aligned content, and a Space Grotesk h1 at 100px/100px, weight 500, with −2px tracking. A nested phrase receives the lime token. The paragraph uses Jost at 18px/26px. Action and portrait reviews form the lower row. **RECONSTRUCTION RECOMMENDATION:** Preserve the broad horizon and keep the brightest photographic area away from the headline. Set white text over a dark overlay; reserve lime for the final phrase and arrow tile. Use flexible line breaks and stack supporting content on small screens. Entrance blur and translation are declared initial states; playback is unverified.

## 2. Contact button with inset arrow

**SOURCE DECLARED:** Button variants include Green/Black and White/green. The official hero shows a lime contact action with a dark rounded arrow tile; the lower action reverses this relationship. Jost supplies lettering; the hero action is 19px/30px, weight 500. **RECONSTRUCTION RECOMMENDATION:** Build one semantic link with a label and decorative arrow. Start with 16px internal spacing and a 44px minimum tap target. Keep the rectangle clickable and add a focus ring. Route contact actions to the declared contact page. Labels alone do not establish quote submission or purchase processing.

## 3. Company capability triptych

**SOURCE DECLARED:** About Company combines explanatory text and a counter, a tall installer photograph with circular badge, and a checklist with an about action. The official reference makes the counter oversized and the image softly rounded. Section display text follows the 45px/55px Space Grotesk role. **RECONSTRUCTION RECOMMENDATION:** Use three columns with aligned tops and deliberately different information densities. Set text in the first column, image in the center, and six concise capabilities in the third. Start with 24px gaps and 24px image corners. Stack in reading order below the tablet band. Treat numerical counters and funding language as template proof requiring approval; do not convert the pictured value into a verified company metric.

## 4. Team portrait tile

**SOURCE DECLARED:** Members contains a dark introduction tile, portrait cards, a Hover layer, Social Icons, and vertical identity text. The official reference shows a lime overlay with portrait still visible, identity at the lower left, and social icons along the right edge. **RECONSTRUCTION RECOMMENDATION:** Use consistently cropped portraits in equal rounded cards. Give the intro tile the same footprint as a portrait. Reveal identity through a lime translucent surface while retaining adequate contrast. Provide the same details through keyboard focus and a persistent mobile treatment. Hover animation timing is unverified. Use real names, roles and approved profile destinations before launch.

## 5. Experience-and-answer module

**SOURCE DECLARED:** CTA Faqs pairs experience imagery with four question rows and explicit Open/Closed, Plus/Minus structures. The question role is Space Grotesk 22px/30px, weight 600. **RECONSTRUCTION RECOMMENDATION:** Keep the visual panel beside a vertical accordion on desktop and place it above the questions on mobile. Implement each question as a button with expanded state, a controlled answer panel and generous clickable padding. Preserve the restrained dividers and icon change. Verify keyboard operation independently because source variants do not establish behavior. Rewrite installation, weather and maintenance answers for the actual service territory and offer; source claims remain illustrative.
