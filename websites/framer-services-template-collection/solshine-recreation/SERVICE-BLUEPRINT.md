# Solshine — homeowner service blueprint

## Homeowner decision path

**SOURCE DECLARED:** The homepage moves from a photographic solar hero and review cluster through client imagery, benefits, service carousel, company capabilities, further services, team, pricing, text bands, service panels, partners, news, FAQs and footer. Contact links provide an enquiry route. Service links distinguish rooftop installation, maintenance, cleaning, off-grid systems, inverter repair and wind repair.

**OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION:** Help homeowners establish service fit, understand installation, inspect proof and request a property-specific conversation. Explain roof assessment, design, installation and aftercare. Clarify territory and consultation scope. Keep contact actions consistent.

## Offer and proof

**SOURCE DECLARED:** The design contains warranty and maintenance benefits, a capability checklist, team portraits, an experience panel, partner logos and review notation. These are template statements and displayed fixtures; this inspection does not verify credentials, review authenticity, warranty coverage or results. Pricing panels contain software-style features and monthly fixture prices, so they do not establish solar installation pricing.

**OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION:** Replace fixtures with approved installer information, warranty terms and permissioned project photography. State property type, scope and completion context. Publish performance figures with their measurement basis and evidence. Address cost without inventing guaranteed savings.

## Content schema

**SOURCE DECLARED:** Named post cards and service-detail routes support reusable content patterns; live CMS configuration is unverified.

**OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION:** Define Service fields for title, slug, summary, hero image, scope, process, exclusions, service area, related questions and contact destination. Define Project fields for title, location granularity, property type, approved images, scope and evidence notes. Add Team fields for name, role, portrait and approved social links. Articles need title, slug, image, excerpt and read-time text; FAQs need question, answer, category and ordering.

## Enquiry form and states

**SOURCE DECLARED:** Homepage contact CTAs link to `/contact-us`; the inspected homepage footer has a required email newsletter input. No homeowner quote-form submission or backend result was verified.

**OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION:** A quote form can request name, email or phone, postcode, service interest and a short project description. Design empty, focused, invalid, submitting, successful and failed states. Preserve entries after errors, provide specific inline guidance, and announce outcomes accessibly. Connect the form only after delivery ownership and response expectations are established; the design specification alone does not promise operational lead handling.
