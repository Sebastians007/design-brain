# Renovation site blueprint

## Site anatomy and visitor decision

SOURCE DECLARED: this is a renovation/remodel company reference with an editorial residential portfolio. Preserve top availability bar and floating bottom navigation → trust-and-image serif hero → architectural image gallery → project manager/credibility introduction → about company → service accordion with image cards → residential project cards → reviews → FAQ → enquiry contact → oversized footer wordmark. Serif Times New Roman headings, Inter body text and the isolated Dancing Script booking accent are separate roles. The official images show composition, while their tilted device framing is promotional presentation.

OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION: adapt it around a homeowner deciding whether the contractor fits their renovation scope. Explain which project types are accepted, the geographical coverage and the next step before presenting an estimate promise. Preserve the portfolio-led storytelling instead of adding emergency dispatch or a generic subscription pricing grid.

## Routes and content records

SOURCE DECLARED: the home includes #hero, #more-info, #about-us, #services, #projects, #reviews, #faqs and #contact IDs. Navigation Projects targets /projects#my-projects; project cards link to individual case routes. The kitchen-remodel case route was inspected as public HTML/CSS and recorded in secondary-declarations.json. Other destination links are declarations only. Several prominent CTAs target a remix link, and the calendar control targets generic Calendly; these do not demonstrate a contractor lead workflow.

OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION: model services with title, description, illustration/photo, eligibility notes and quote destination. Model projects with slug, title, room/project category, authorized photography, factual scope and approved client attribution. Preserve the source case-page anatomy; add schedule/budget fields only when reliable content exists. Replace demo office, phone, testimonials and counters with approved company details. Do not publish sample personal attribution as genuine customer evidence.

## Responsive delivery and states

SOURCE DECLARED: page variants break at 1200px and 810px. Hero typography is 74px base and 42px compact. Section h2 uses 48/36/32px; hero phone padding uses 18px horizontal edges. Accordion, FAQ, CTA hover/no-hover and navigation phone variants exist. Their browser interaction behavior and exact timing remain unobserved.

OPTIONAL RECOMMENDATION: provide menu open/closed, service selected/expanded, FAQ expanded/collapsed and form idle/invalid/sending/success/error states. On small screens keep heading fragments coherent, images in reading order and the floating navigation away from submit controls. Use static availability when motion is reduced, and preserve text while images load. A successful enquiry requires a configured endpoint and tested delivery; this source study verifies neither submission nor calendar availability.
