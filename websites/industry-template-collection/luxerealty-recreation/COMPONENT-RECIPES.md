# LuxeRealty component recipes

Evidence: FTC Studio's official LuxeRealty listing, three downloaded official reference JPEGs, saved home HTML/CSS and the inspected public `/properties/listing` source. The official references show the actual hero inside Marketplace monitor/laptop mockups; recreate the page content, not the surrounding device or room. SOURCE DECLARED labels identify CSS/HTML facts. OPTIONAL recommendations are implementation choices. Filtering, saved-property persistence, form delivery and current sales data are unverified.

## 1. Translucent navigation with two discovery tools

SOURCE DECLARED: navigation groups point to Properties, About, Agents and Blogs, with search and a liked-property heart at the right. Properties resolves to `/properties/listing`, while the heart resolves to `/liked-properties`. The wordmark declares BT Suave Regular, 24px/1.2em at weight 500; the interface labels use Riviera Nights Trial Regular, 14px/150% with uppercase treatment. Desktop, Phone and Tablet variants exist. OPTIONAL: build the wordmark, primary links and icon actions as separate alignment groups; provide visible names for search and liked properties, a clear focus state and a menu trigger controlling the compact links. Keep icon hit areas comfortably operable without enlarging their visible line drawings. Preserve the distinction between the elegant logo face and the neutral uppercase navigation.

## 2. Opening sequence of estate scenes

SOURCE DECLARED: New Property List contains a Hero Section introducing “The Art of Fine Living.” and four named property scenes: La Mer Oceanfront, The Dynasty Manor, Orion Bayfront and The Summit. Each has a photographic media wrapper, a white content card, location and Explore destination. The hero h1 preset is 46px/1.2em with -2.6px tracking; scene h2 is 32px/1.1em with -.01em tracking. Official references show tall centered white title panels against room/exterior photographs. OPTIONAL: make each photograph's focal crop independent of its card. Preserve the complete sequence in reading order, with a functional property link even if scroll effects are unavailable. Treat the staged estate/location names as demo copy, and reconcile them against approved property records before publication. Do not infer transition timing from a static image.

## 3. Inventory cards and the separate filter destination

SOURCE DECLARED: Featured Properties anchors at `#featured-properties` and links to `/properties/listing`. Cards provide lifestyle/category, location, title, price and beds/baths; desktop titles use Riviera Nights Trial Medium 18px/140%, changing to 16px in compact CSS presets. The inspected listings page declares a Filters heading, Lifestyle_Checkbox and Location_Checkbox components, repeated Property Card records and a compact “Filter properties” button. OPTIONAL: create one card data model shared by home and inventory; keep currency and bed/bath labels explicit. Use a deterministic predicate for selected lifestyle/location values and update a result count without moving focus. Define no-results and clear-filter states. Search may be implemented where the source search control calls for it, but do not add unsupported filters simply because they are common in real estate sites. The saved source establishes controls, not their successful behavior.

## 4. Agency story, agents and commercial proof

SOURCE DECLARED: About Luxe is followed by Agents, Luxe Realty, Our Expertise, Featured In and Global Reach. Agents declares a purple radial background; the Luxe Realty scene has a purple surface with a large white “Luxe” word, whose inline declarations include 160px/1.2em, -7.4px tracking and a separate 99px variant. Agent cards carry a Licensed label, portrait, name, email and profile destination. Our Expertise has Active/Inactive structures and associated images; Global Reach groups continents, licensed agents and total projects. OPTIONAL: let the oversized word respond to its own container after fonts load, and retain portrait/name relationships when cards stack. Keep media and explanation together in each expertise state. Substitute only approved people, licensing descriptions and metrics; source labels are demonstration content, not verified credentials.

## 5. Editorial cards, newsletter and contact footer

SOURCE DECLARED: Blogs anchors at `#blogs`; article cards carry category and title. Newsletter anchors at `#newsletter` and presents Join the Elite with an email field and Send action. Contact/footer structures include first name, last name, email and phone controls, a large wordmark, property/agent destinations, About, Blogs and social links. OPTIONAL: use separate form state machines for newsletter and sales enquiry, with inline validation, sending, success and retry messages. Keep the form labels visible and preserve entered values after an error. Group footer routes in the source sequence on phone. Remove duplicate responsive IDs when reconstructing the source's repeated `#global-reach` branches; one accessible section target should own that name.
