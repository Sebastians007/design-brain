05

Frame Rules

Beat · One Word · Small Overlays

Do

  • Hard-cut every clip on a beat; one phrase and one caption per clip.
  • Keep overlays small: a tag chip top-left, a bold caption bottom-centre.
  • Pick the light or dark chip variant to match the clip brightness.
  • Keep one light moment (the cold open); the rest is dark.
  • Put the blue-to-green gradient on the end-card wordmark.

Don't

  • Don't crossfade the reel.
  • Don't let overlays cover the clip subject.
  • Don't caption text already on screen.
  • Don't add pills or shadows behind reel captions.
  • Don't add effects that the trailer does not show.
