05

Frame Rules

Calm · Workspace-Real · Clay

Do

  • Keep the cream ground in the opening, statement and end frames.
  • Show one window or one statement per scene.
  • Use clay as the only emphasis: stat numerals, one word, caret, scrubber.
  • Hold the preview card layout identical between scenes; swap only the panel.
  • Crossfade (about 0.3s) between scenes.

Don't

  • Don't add a second accent colour.
  • Don't use glow, grain or shader transitions.
  • Don't use hard cuts where a short crossfade fits.
  • Don't put more than one window on a frame.
