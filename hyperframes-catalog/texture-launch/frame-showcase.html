05

Frame Rules

Centred · Flat · Rhythmic

Do

  • Keep one centred thing on screen at a time.
  • Set captions as heavy black Inter on paper, revealed word by word.
  • Pair a texture word with a collage tile and two flat colour blocks.
  • Nudge the textured word every ~0.1s so it reads as a flipbook.

Don’t

  • Don’t mix paper captions and a colour stage in the same frame.
  • Don’t show more than one textured word at once.
  • Don’t decorate without rhythm.
  • Don’t lose contrast between the texture word and its stage.
